Bisi: Baby First Foods & BLW – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bisi is a guide and tracker for starting solids. This policy explains what information the app stores and what happens to it. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we do not collect any data. Questions: razleshem3@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

Bisi has no account, no sign-up and no server. The information you enter is saved only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone:

  • Baby profile: the name you type, date of birth, weeks born early (optional), eczema severity and known food allergies (optional), family food rules (for example vegetarian, kosher, halal), and whether you confirmed a doctor’s OK for peanut.
  • Food log: the foods you log, dates, reactions and the details you type (symptoms, timing, notes), readiness-checklist ticks, skipped or cleared foods, milestones, meal-planner weeks and shopping-list ticks.
  • Settings: language, appearance, region, units and the country used for emergency numbers.

The app does not ask for photos, contacts, location, the microphone, the camera or Apple Health, and has no such permissions.

What we collect

Nothing. The app makes no network requests: it has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see what you enter. The App Store privacy label for Bisi is “Data Not Collected”.

Purchases

Bisi Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment and holds your subscription status; we receive no personal or payment information. Apple’s handling of that data is described in Apple’s privacy policy. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store on your device to decide whether Plus features are unlocked.

Sharing

We share nothing because we hold nothing. Two actions let you move data off the device, and only when you choose to: printing or saving the pediatrician report as a PDF (Plus) and “Share as text”, which hands the report to the iOS share sheet. Links to sources, first-aid courses and emergency numbers open in Safari or the Phone app; those sites have their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your phone until you remove it. In the app, open Settings and tap Erase all data on this phone, or delete the app. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, the app’s data is part of that backup under Apple’s terms; delete the backup to remove it there.

Children

Bisi is made for parents and caregivers and is rated for adults, not for use by children. It is not directed to children under 13 and it does not create an account for the baby or for anyone else. The baby information you enter is stored only on your device, as described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Bisi provides general information based on published research and guidelines. It is not medical advice and does not replace your pediatrician, dietitian or allergist. Talk to your doctor about your baby, especially before introducing allergens if your baby has eczema or a known food allergy. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Because the app does not collect data, changes would most likely describe new optional features (for example a backup or export).
