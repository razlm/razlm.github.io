Need help? Email razleshem3@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version and a short description. We aim to reply within a few days.

Bisi is not medical advice.
Bisi shares general information based on published guidelines. It does not replace your pediatrician, dietitian or allergist. If your baby has eczema, a known food allergy, or you are unsure, talk to your doctor before starting solids. If your baby is having a reaction right now, call your local emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Bisi Plus purchase?
Open Settings (the gear icon at the top of the Today screen) → Bisi Plus → Restore purchases. The same button is at the bottom of the Plus screen. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.
How do I cancel Bisi Plus?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone open Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Bisi → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep Plus until the period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
What is free and what is Plus?
Free: the full food database (age, serving by age, choking risk, allergen flags), the plan, the food log and reaction log, allergen coverage, progress and milestones, the starter recipes, all Learn guides and everything in Safety. Bisi Plus (optional subscription) adds the weekly meal planner with shopping list, the allergen rotation calendar and reminders, the printable pediatrician report, nutrient insights, recipe packs and profiles for more than one baby. Safety content is never paid.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
Does Bisi work offline?
Yes, completely. Only the App Store purchase and restore need a connection.
Is my data backed up? What if I get a new phone?
Your data lives only on this device, inside the app. It is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a backup to a new phone brings it along. There is no cloud sync and no export yet. The pediatrician report (Plus) can be printed, saved as a PDF or shared as text.
How do I delete my data?
Open Settings in the app and tap Erase all data on this phone. Deleting the app also removes all of its data. There is nothing to delete on our side because we never receive it.
The app says a food is “recommended from N months” but my doctor said something else.
Follow your doctor. Starting ages in Bisi are hints based on published guidelines, never locks: every food can be logged at any age.
Can I use Bisi in Hebrew?
Yes. Tap the language button at the top of any screen, or change it in Settings. Hebrew is shown right-to-left.
How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?
Email us. Screenshots help a lot.
