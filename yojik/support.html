Yojik: Hedgehog Runner – Support

Need help? Email razleshem3@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version and a short description. We aim to reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
I bought coins or a skin but did not get it.
Tap Restore Purchases in the Shop and wait a few seconds with an internet connection. If it still does not appear, email us with your Apple receipt or order ID.
Do I need an account?
No. An anonymous profile is created automatically so your coins, skins and leaderboard score can be saved.
Does the game work offline?
Yes, you can play offline. Leaderboard and purchase syncing need a connection.
How do I delete my data?
Email us from the address you contact us with and mention your leaderboard name, and we will delete the profile and its data. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.
Will I keep my progress on a new phone?
Progress is stored in an anonymous profile on your device. Deleting the app or switching phones starts you fresh. Skins you bought with real money come back with Restore Purchases. Sign-in to keep progress across devices is planned for a future update.
How do I report an inappropriate leaderboard name?
Use the Report a name link under the leaderboard, or email us with the name and score. Names are filtered for obvious profanity, and we remove offending names promptly.
How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?
Email us. Screenshots help a lot.
