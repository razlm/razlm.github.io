Yojik: Hedgehog Runner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Yojik is a free arcade game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Questions: razleshem3@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Anonymous user ID. A random ID is created automatically so your coins, skins, streak and scores can be saved. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Leaderboard name (optional). If you type a name (letters and numbers, up to 10 characters), it appears on the leaderboard. Names are filtered for obvious profanity and you can report a name with the link under the leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown.
  • Purchase information. When you buy something, Apple processes the payment. We receive a purchase confirmation (product and transaction ID) to unlock your items. We never see your payment details.
  • Gameplay events. Basic events such as runs started, scores, rewards claimed and purchases viewed, used to fix bugs and balance the game.
  • Advertising (only in versions that show ads). If ads are shown, the ad provider may process device identifiers and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Ads are optional rewards you choose to watch, or occasional interstitials. You can remove ads with an in-app purchase.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or health data.

How we use it

To run the game (save progress, leaderboard, purchases), to understand and improve the game, and, where applicable, to show ads. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

Data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and, where ads are used, shared with the ad provider (for example Google AdMob). Apple handles purchases and the App Store. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your profile while the game is in use. You can ask us to delete your profile and its data at any time by emailing us. Deleting the app removes data stored on your device.

Your choices

You can change or clear your leaderboard name at any time in the game. Because there is no sign-in, progress lives on your device and is lost if you delete the app. To have your profile and its data removed from our server, email us.

Children

Yojik is rated 9+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
